# Example: Parallel Fibre (fictional)

Parallel Fibre is a **fictional** challenger broadband provider in the UK. Every number below is invented to show how the system fits together.

**Context:** high-consideration purchase (18 to 24 month contracts), price-sensitive buyers who compare on aggregator sites, a long decision cycle, and CAC under pressure from larger incumbents. Paid media alone could not close the gap; the team switched from channel-by-channel optimisation to one full-funnel backlog.

## Diagnostic summary (last 8 weeks)

**Top three leaks:** mobile address step (friction), plan comparison (clarity), first-bill contacts (expectation mismatch).
